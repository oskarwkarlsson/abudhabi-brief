A tanker is hit leaving Hormuz as Iran claims three ships struck mines

UKMTO confirmed a tanker was hit by an unknown projectile near Khasab, Oman, at about 17:00 CET Thursday, with no crew injuries reported. Iran's claim that three tankers hit mines is not independently verified. No attack on UAE territory was reported, and the US, UK and Swedish advice for the UAE is unchanged.

UKMTO bekräftade att ett tankfartyg träffades av en okänd projektil nära Khasab i Oman vid 17.00 CET i torsdags. Inga skadade i besättningen har rapporterats. Tom Barrack told CNN that Qatar and Pakistan have helped bring the two sides close to agreeing a de-escalation process, though he said a pause would not end the conflict. An Iranian official recently called the latest Qatari ideas "unacceptable", so this is hopeful but not settled.

Health · Hälsa

UAE recalls a baby gripe water over undeclared alcohol

The Emirates Drug Establishment and Dubai Health Authority recalled Babies' Magic Tea Baby Sleep Gripe Water, sold for colic and sleep in babies over two weeks old. US FDA tests found about 8.3 per cent ethanol plus methanol and isopropanol. Worth remembering with a newborn on the way: stop using it and discard or return it.

The National →

Emiraten återkallar ett kolikmedel för spädbarn på grund av odeklarerad alkohol

Emirates Drug Establishment och Dubai Health Authority har återkallat Babies' Magic Tea Baby Sleep Gripe Water, som säljs mot kolik och för bättre sömn hos spädbarn från två veckors ålder. Amerikanska FDA:s tester visade cirka 8,3 procent etanol samt metanol och isopropanol. Bra att komma ihåg med en bebis på väg: sluta använda produkten och släng den eller lämna tillbaka den.

Abu Dhabi villa rents average AED 258,000 a year and are flat on the quarter

ValuStrat's Q3 review puts citywide villa asking rents at AED 258,000 a year, up 1.8 per cent on last year and stable since Q2, while the 0 per cent cap on rent increases has applied since June 2. A useful benchmark for your villa budget.
