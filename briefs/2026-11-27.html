Iranian drones kill two Italian workers in Kuwait; UAE advice unchanged

Kuwait's defence ministry says falling shrapnel from intercepted Iranian drones aimed at a vital site killed two Italians on Thursday and injured a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani. No attack on the UAE was reported. The US stays at Level 3, FCDO is unchanged since November 18 and UD has no avrådan.

Ingen attack mot Emiraten rapporterades. USA ligger kvar på nivå 3, FCDO är oförändrat sedan 18 november och UD har ingen avrådan.

Lufthansa and Eurowings are flying to Dubai again

Since Wednesday, November 25, Lufthansa flies Munich to Dubai five times a week and Eurowings flies daily from Berlin; Air France has flown Paris to Dubai daily since November 19. Frankfurt, Zurich and Vienna flights stay suspended until March 27, 2027, and BA's Abu Dhabi route not until winter 2027 to 2028. Useful when planning the post New Year visit.

Health · Hälsa

Having a baby in the UAE: the paperwork in order

The National's guide: bring both passports, Emirates IDs and an attested marriage certificate to hospital. A newborn is generally covered by the mother's insurance for 30 days, then needs its own policy. The passport comes first, then the residence visa and Emirates ID, which can be done remotely. Birth certificates cost 50 AED per language via TAMM in Abu Dhabi.

The National →

Att få barn i Emiraten: pappersarbetet i rätt ordning

The Nationals guide: ta med båda passen, Emirates ID och ett legaliserat vigselbevis till sjukhuset. Ett nyfött barn omfattas i regel av moderns försäkring i 30 dagar och behöver sedan en egen. Passet kommer först, sedan uppehållstillstånd och Emirates ID, som kan ordnas på distans. Födelsebevis kostar 50 AED per språk via TAMM i Abu Dhabi.
