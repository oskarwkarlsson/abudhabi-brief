US reportedly tells Gulf allies to prepare for possible renewed strikes on Iran after Nov 3

Two diplomats say US officials warned regional allies of possible action after the midterms and of possible Iranian retaliation, while JD Vance wrote to Iran's security chief about talks. No decision has been made and no attack on UAE territory was reported. The US stays at Level 3, and neither the UK nor Sweden changed its advice.

January 7 becomes Zayed the Founder Day

President Sheikh Mohamed declared a new annual national occasion honouring Sheikh Zayed, first marked on January 7, 2027. That falls around your planned visit after New Year, so expect events and possibly busier landmarks; no day off has been announced.

Safety · Säkerhet

Oil spill closes all beaches in Sharjah, Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah

Sharjah shut every beach on Wednesday as a precaution, joining its two northern neighbours. The tanker and cause are not officially confirmed, and the environment ministry says the fuel smell poses no health risk. No Abu Dhabi beach closures were reported.

The National →

Oljeutsläpp stänger alla stränder i Sharjah, Umm Al Quwain och Ras Al Khaimah

Sharjah stängde i onsdags alla sina stränder som en försiktighetsåtgärd, precis som de två grannemiraten i norr redan gjort. Vilket tankfartyg det gäller och vad som orsakade utsläppet är inte officiellt bekräftat, och miljöministeriet säger att bränslelukten inte innebär någon hälsorisk. Inga stängda stränder har rapporterats i Abu Dhabi.

Money · Pengar

The Fed holds rates, and the dirham's peg means UAE rates usually follow

The US Federal Reserve kept rates unchanged on Wednesday. Because the dirham is pegged to the dollar, UAE mortgage and savings rates track Fed moves, which matters for car loans and any deposit you park in AED.
