SafetySafety & regional security

What touches the UAE, factual and calm. FCDO, US State Dept and Swedish UD advisories when they change.

  • Advisories. The US stays at Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" for the UAE, last issued Oct 23, with no change since (travel.state.gov). The UK FCDO page is unchanged since Jul 24 (FCDO). Sweden's UD still has no avrådan for the UAE, removed on Jul 3 (Regeringen).
  • Diplomacy. Saudi, Pakistani and Turkish officials met Iranian leaders in Tehran on Oct 26 and agreed to continue talks. Iran's Ali Bagheri Kani said Washington must meet seven conditions first. Oman's foreign minister called for a non-aggression pact among the six GCC states, Iraq and Iran. Gulf News
  • Saudi and shipping. The Saudi-led coalition intercepted four Houthi drones aimed at the south and three ballistic missiles aimed at the Eastern region. UKMTO reported a vessel on fire about 63 nautical miles southeast of Al-Faw in Iraq, in the northern Gulf, not in UAE waters or Hormuz; the cause is unconfirmed. None of the sources checked reported an attack on UAE territory. Gulf News
  • Flights. Etihad's Abu Dhabi flights to Riyadh and Dammam stay cancelled through Nov 5, along with selected Jeddah flights. Emirates plans to resume Dubai to Jeddah on Oct 30 and Riyadh and Dammam on Nov 8. Flights by Iranian airlines to the UAE stay suspended. Gulf News
