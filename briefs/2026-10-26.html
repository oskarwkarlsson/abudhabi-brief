What touches the UAE, factual and calm. FCDO, US State Dept and Swedish UD advisories when they change.

RulesRules, visas & legislation

Golden Visa and residency, labour and family law, licence conversion, health insurance.

  • Notice periods explained. Under the UAE private-sector labour law, notice runs from 30 to 90 days, the same for both sides, and whoever skips it pays the unserved wages. Leaving during probation has its own rules, including a possible one-year work-permit ban. Your ADIA contract may set its own terms, so treat this as background. Gulf News
