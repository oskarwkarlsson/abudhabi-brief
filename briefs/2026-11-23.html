Two hurt in Bahrain and a tanker incident in the Gulf of Oman; no attack on the UAE

Bahrain said two people were injured in Saturday's Iranian attack on an industrial site, one of them critically. UKMTO reported a tanker incident involving "military forces" in the Gulf of Oman near Chabahar on Sunday evening; a maritime analyst said the US struck a tanker carrying Iranian oil, which neither the US nor Iran has confirmed unconfirmed. Bahrain uppgav att två personer skadades i lördagens iranska attack mot en industrianläggning, en av dem allvarligt. Housing · Boende

Yas villa asking rents up 32% this year, while the rent freeze holds

Property Finder data prepared for Emarat Al Youm show average asking rents for Yas Island villas up 32.3% in January to October against a year earlier, to 421,480 AED a year, and Al Reem villas up 27.45% to 258,760 AED portal data. These are listing prices, not registered contracts, and ADREC's temporary 0% cap, which ties renewals and new agreements to the last registered Tawtheeq rent, still applies. Worth keeping the gap in mind when we negotiate a villa.

Begärda villahyror på Yas upp 32 procent i år, medan hyresstoppet ligger kvar

Data från Property Finder, framtagna för Emarat Al Youm, visar att den genomsnittliga begärda hyran för villor på Yas Island steg med 32,3 procent under januari till oktober jämfört med ett år tidigare, till 421 480 AED per år, och för villor på Al Reem med 27,45 procent till 258 760 AED portaldata. Det är annonspriser, inte registrerade kontrakt, och ADREC:s tillfälliga tak på 0 procent, som knyter förlängningar och nya avtal till den senast registrerade hyran i Tawtheeq, gäller fortfarande. Värt att ha skillnaden i minnet när vi förhandlar om en villa.

UAE drug authority warns parents about GTA 6

The National Drug Enforcement Authority warned that Grand Theft Auto 6, launched on Thursday, shows drug use and other illegal activity that may affect children and teenagers, and urged age-appropriate parental controls. Jordan is moving to ban the game. It is the second child safety warning about games in two days, after Roblox chat staying off in the UAE.
