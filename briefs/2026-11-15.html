Bahrain issues a nationwide alert; no attack on the UAE

Bahrain's Interior Ministry issued its first nationwide threat alert in months early on Sunday, urging people to stay calm and go to the nearest safe location, after Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed drone attacks on US targets at Bahrain's Riffa helicopter base. Washington has not confirmed the latest claims; on Friday US Central Command said it intercepted two Iranian drones heading for Bahrain with no damage. No attack on UAE territory was reported and advisory levels are unchanged.

Bahrains inrikesministerium utfärdade tidigt på söndagen sin första landsomfattande hotvarning på flera månader och uppmanade invånarna att hålla sig lugna och ta sig till närmaste säkra plats, sedan Irans revolutionsgarde hävdat drönarattacker mot amerikanska mål vid helikopterbasen Riffa i Bahrain. Prison, Dh300,000 fines and deportation over the Yas hotel brawl

The Abu Dhabi Criminal Court convicted 10 people over Wednesday's brawl at the Yas Hilton involving fighters Usman Nurmagomedov and Merab Dvalishvili. Six got six months in prison, a Dh300,000 fine each and deportation; two were fined Dh50,000 each; and two who filmed and published the footage got the same six months, Dh300,000 and deportation for violating privacy. A sharp reminder for all of us, teenagers included, never to film or post strangers in an incident here.

Riksdag votes on November 25 on a 160-day tax residence rule

The Riksdag's tax committee is finalising its report on the government bill defining "stadigvarande vistelse" (habitual stay): more than 160 days in Sweden in a calendar year, or more than 120 days in two consecutive years. The chamber debates it on November 24 and decides on November 25; the bill proposes a start on January 1, 2027. Worth tracking for how many days we can spend back home after the move without becoming fully taxable in Sweden again.

Riksdagen röstar den 25 november om en skatteregel på 160 dagar

Riksdagens skatteutskott färdigställer sitt betänkande om regeringens proposition som definierar "stadigvarande vistelse": fler än 160 dagar i Sverige under ett kalenderår, eller fler än 120 dagar två år i rad. Kammaren debatterar den 24 november och beslutar den 25 november; propositionen föreslår att reglerna börjar gälla den 1 januari 2027. Värt att följa för hur många dagar vi kan vara hemma efter flytten utan att åter bli obegränsat skattskyldiga i Sverige.
