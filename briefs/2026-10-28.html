Stricter UAE driving test rules from January 2027

Under Cabinet Decision No. 174 of 2026, learners can fail the practical test for a dangerous mistake even with the 75% pass mark. This only matters if one of you has to sit a UAE test, so check early whether your Swedish licences can be exchanged directly.

SafetySafety & regional security

What touches the UAE, factual and calm. FCDO, US State Dept and Swedish UD advisories when they change.

  • Advisories. The US stays at Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" for the UAE, last issued Oct 23, with no change since (travel.state.gov). The UK FCDO did update its UAE page at 17:03 CET on Oct 27, rewording the regional tensions section on its "Warnings and insurance" page. It says the risk of sudden escalation is high and tells British nationals in or travelling through the region to expect possible flight cancellations and airspace closures. FCDO still does not advise against travel to the UAE, and the same update went out for more than a dozen countries in the region (FCDO). Sweden's UD still has no avrådan for the UAE, removed on Jul 3 (Regeringen).
  • Possible US operations. CNN reports US forces could launch major operations against Iran within 96 hours of an order. The Wall Street Journal reports Washington warned Gulf governments of a possible "short, intense renewal" of the war after the Nov 3 midterms. No decision has been made. Gulf News, The National unnamed officials
  • Saudi Arabia. Saudi Arabia says it destroyed five Houthi ballistic missiles. Debris damaged a building, a construction site and a car in Riyadh, with no casualties reported. The coalition also intercepted a missile aimed at Taif. None of the sources checked reported an attack on UAE territory. Gulf News, The National
  • Flights and Hormuz. Etihad's Abu Dhabi flights to Riyadh, Dammam and Jeddah stay cancelled through Nov 5. UAE departing seats rose 23% between June and October.
