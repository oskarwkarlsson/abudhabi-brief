Iran says two more tankers and a tug caught fire near Hormuz

The IRGC said on Thursday that one tanker hit a mine on an "unauthorized" southern route and another supertanker and a tugboat helping it caught fire, and that it captured two US drones. The claims could not be independently verified and there was no immediate US comment. unconfirmed Nothing was reported on UAE territory.

Trump and Vance say the Iran war cost the midterms

Vance wrote that "the reason we lost is the war", and Trump called that "largely true" but said he would do it again. Analysts quoted say Gulf states welcome the breathing space but remain anxious about a return to war; Democrats take Congress in January and plan to scrutinise war funding.
