Jail and deportation for posting a child's video without consent

A Dubai court gave a woman one month in prison followed by deportation for posting a video of her five-year-old relative on public Snapchat without the parents' consent, under the cybercrime law and Wadeema's child rights law. Worth knowing for family gatherings and school events.

Villa rents rose in H1, led by Saadiyat and Khalifa City

Bayut's Abu Dhabi report (updated Nov 6) says average villa asking rents rose 7% on Saadiyat, 4% on Yas and 7% in Khalifa City versus H2 2025. Since June 3 the ADREC 0% cap means renewals and new leases use the last Tawtheeq rent. Portal data, so treat it as a guide.
