Shipping attacks around Hormuz keep mounting

UKMTO reported another tanker incident in the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, with crew status and damage unknown, less than a day after a tanker was hit by a projectile off Oman. The IMO counts 133 confirmed maritime incidents in the region as of November 10, with 25 seafarers killed. No attack on UAE land was reported and advisory levels are unchanged.

New child car seat rules from January 1, 2027

Under Cabinet Decision No. 174 of 2026, children aged four and under must ride in a suitable, approved child seat in the rear. A child may sit in the front passenger seat only if older than 10 or at least 145 cm tall. Both our children fall under the car seat rule, so plan seats for both cars.

Nya regler för bilbarnstolar från 1 januari 2027

Enligt regeringsbeslut nr 174 från 2026 ska barn som är fyra år eller yngre åka i en lämplig, godkänd bilbarnstol i baksätet. Ett barn får sitta i passagerarsätet fram bara om det är äldre än 10 år eller minst 145 cm långt. Båda våra barn omfattas av kravet på bilbarnstol, så planera stolar till båda bilarna.

Heavy rain possible again today, easing after

The NCM forecasts intermittent rain, heavy in places, on Thursday, with north-westerly winds up to 55 km/h, blowing dust and coastal highs of 27 to 31 °C. Conditions should ease after Thursday, though winds up to 45 km/h and rough Gulf seas continue into next week. The Interior Ministry asks drivers to slow down and avoid wadis and beaches.

Kraftigt regn möjligt igen i dag, sedan lugnare

NCM väntar sig periodvis regn, på sina håll kraftigt, på torsdag, med nordvästliga vindar på upp till 55 km/h, blåsande damm och dagstemperaturer vid kusten på 27 till 31 °C. Vädret väntas lugna sig efter torsdag, men vindar på upp till 45 km/h och grov sjö i Persiska viken fortsätter in i nästa vecka. More family-friendly spaces and services planned

At the UAE Government Annual Meetings on Tuesday, officials set priorities under the National Family Growth Agenda 2031: adapting public spaces for families and weighing family needs in policy and infrastructure. The Family First programme already brings priority parking for expectant mothers, family seating on public transport and Family & Child Oasis Rooms for feeding and caring for infants.
