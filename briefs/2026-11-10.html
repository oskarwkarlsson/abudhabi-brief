Rules · Regler

UAE postpones the planned five-vehicle limit per resident

The Ministry of Interior has put on hold the Article 89 cap, part of the federal traffic rules due on January 1, 2027, which would have allowed five vehicles per person with extra fees for each one beyond the first two. A review will weigh the needs of families, investors and owners of classic cars. Two family cars were never near the cap.

Emiraten skjuter upp det planerade taket på fem fordon per invånare

Inrikesministeriet har pausat taket i artikel 89, en del av de federala trafikreglerna som ska gälla från den 1 januari 2027, som skulle ha tillåtit fem fordon per person med extra avgifter för varje fordon utöver de två första. En översyn ska väga in behoven hos familjer, investerare och ägare av klassiska bilar. Två familjebilar låg aldrig i närheten av taket.

Abu Dhabi asks everyone to report child safety concerns

The Early Childhood Authority's "Protecting Children Takes All of Us" campaign runs until mid-2027 through schools, majlis and media. Reports can be anonymous and need no proof: Family Care Authority hotline 800444, the Ministry of Interior child protection line 116111, or police on 999. Useful numbers for parents of young children.
