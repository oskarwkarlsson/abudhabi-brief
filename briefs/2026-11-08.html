US Mission issues a sharper security alert for the region

The Nov 7 alert asks Americans in the Middle East for heightened vigilance over an increased threat of armed attacks by Iranian-supported Houthis and Iranian efforts to target US officials, and warns of possible flight cancellations and airspace closures. The advisory level itself did not change. Worth noting for the after-New Year visit: keep bookings flexible.

US widens its Iran squeeze as Tehran lifts missile range limits

CENTCOM launched Task Force Iron Gate on Nov 7 to disrupt Iranian weapons routes over land and the Caspian, and disabled the M/V Huda in the Gulf of Oman. Iran said it was lifting limits on its ballistic missile ranges. Qatar and others keep mediating. No attack on UAE territory was reported.

Housing · Boende

Abu Dhabi property deals jump 76% in nine months

ADREC says transactions reached Dh171.26 billion through September. Al Reem led on value, followed by Al Hudayriyat, Saadiyat and Yas; those districts plus Zayed City and Khalifa City are expected to provide 77% of new supply through 2030. A busy sales market is useful context for villa rents next spring.

Fastighetsaffärerna i Abu Dhabi ökar med 76 procent på nio månader

ADREC uppger att transaktionerna nådde 171,26 miljarder AED till och med september. Al Reem ledde i värde, följt av Al Hudayriyat, Saadiyat och Yas; de områdena plus Zayed City och Khalifa City väntas stå för 77 procent av det nya utbudet fram till 2030. En livlig försäljningsmarknad är bra bakgrund inför villahyrorna nästa vår.

A Children's Biennale opens at Umm Al Emarat Park

Billed as the Middle East's first outdoor biennale for children, it is part of the 23rd Abu Dhabi Festival, has eight interactive artworks and runs until December 6. A good pick for the 2- and 4-year-old on a future weekend; it ends before your January visit.
