SafetySafety & regional security

What touches the UAE, factual and calm. FCDO, US State Dept and Swedish UD advisories when they change.

  • US staffing. The UAE embassy is on reduced staff, not an ordered departure. A DNI memo of Oct 23 says Iran has "substantially increased efforts to target U.S. personnel". Officials deny any link to possible strikes on Iran. PBS, Axios
  • Advisories. The US updated its UAE advisory on Oct 23. It stays at Level 3 "Reconsider Travel" with no change to the level, and the new text cites Houthi aggression and Iranian targeting of US officials (US Embassy). The Oct 18 US security alert asks for vigilance, warns of possible flight disruption and gives 999 for emergencies (alert). The UK FCDO page is unchanged since Jul 24. Sweden's UD has had no avrådan since Jul 3 (Regeringen).
  • Region. On Sunday Saudi air defences downed a Houthi missile aimed at Riyadh (The National), and Riyadh airport has resumed flights (Gulf News). Etihad has cancelled its Abu Dhabi flights to Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam through Nov 5 (Gulf News). None of the sources checked reported an attack on UAE territory this week.
  • Oil spill. A tanker spill caused the fuel smell reported across the UAE. Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah closed their beaches and Sharjah sealed off Al Hamriya. The reports checked mention no closures in Abu Dhabi. The National

SchoolsSchools & childcare

ADEK rules, fees, admissions, new schools and ratings. British, IB, international and Nordic options, nurseries.

  • Emirati teachers. ADEK's new Emiratization Policy for Private Education aims to bring 2,000+ Emirati teachers into private schools over five years. It starts with Social Studies, Arabic and Islamic Studies, so it hits the core curriculum less at first. Taaleem (200+), SABIS, GEMS, ICS and Liwa have made pledges. ADEK, The National

HousingHousing & neighbourhoods

Villa rents in Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem, Khalifa City and Al Raha. Tenancy rules, Tawtheeq, ADREC.

  • Rent freeze. Since Jun 3 the annual rent increase cap has been 0% instead of 5% for residential, commercial and industrial leases. Tenants can report breaches to ADREC. For your January viewings: ask for the unit's last Tawtheeq rent, because that is the legal reference. ADREC (Jun 3)
  • Savills Q3 (Oct 14). Villa values are up 8% on the year and rental transactions up 29% on the quarter, driven mainly by renewals. New-lease rents were "mixed" across key communities. Apartments are up 9%. Savills

MoneyMoney & cost of living

Fees, utilities, fuel and cars, banking, the AED peg, and Swedish tax, ISK and emigration rules.

  • Fuel. Petrol is expected to rise for a fourth month in November, with prices due around Oct 31. October prices: Super 98 Dh4.40, Special 95 Dh4.28 and diesel Dh4.80 a litre, more than 70% above March. That is still about half the UK price. The National
  • Groceries. Lulu and other retailers pledge to hold staple prices. Dubai food inflation was 8.05% in September. The National
  • Swedish tax (dated Sep 1, worth knowing). A government bill would define "stadigvarande vistelse": more than 160 nights in Sweden in a calendar year, or more than 120 if the year before was also over 120. Only nights slept in Sweden count, and the change would start on Jan 1, 2027. Useful for planning summers at home after you deregister. Regeringen proposal, Riksdag vote pending

RulesRules, visas & legislation

Golden Visa and residency, labour and family law, licence conversion, health insurance.

  • Golden Visa, skilled route. The official criteria: a UAE job at MOHRE Level 1 or 2, an attested bachelor's degree, Dh30,000 a month, an employer salary certificate and 6 months of bank statements (ADRO). VisaHQ and UECN report an ADRO circular dated Oct 19 that makes this explicitly basic salary, excluding allowances (VisaHQ). Tip: get your degree attested before you leave Sweden. circular framing secondary only
  • ICP nomination step. VisaHQ reports that ICP now requires a nomination letter plus a Dh150 fee for Golden Visa files. VisaHQ (Oct 16) single source, unconfirmed

HealthHealth & family

Hospitals, maternity care, paediatrics, vaccinations, DoH rules.

  • Maternity. DoH's Project Cradle, a two-year study with Oura, gives smart rings to high-risk pregnancies to spot preterm-birth warning signs. It runs at Corniche Hospital and Mediclinic Airport Road, two of the main maternity hospitals. The National
